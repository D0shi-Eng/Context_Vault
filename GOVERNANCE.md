# Governance

## Current model

ContextVault is maintained directly in its canonical repository. Repository history and `NOTICE` provide authorship and attribution; public documentation does not assign a redundant ownership title.

## Evidence-based decisions

Architecture, security, compatibility, and release decisions must identify:

- The requirement or threat being addressed.
- The alternatives considered.
- The decision and its consequences.
- The verification evidence.
- Any residual risk or deferred work.

## Release authority

A maintainer must explicitly approve:

- Changing repository visibility.
- Publishing a package or release.
- Enabling network transport or hosted services.
- Changing the license or project identity.
- Accepting a residual Critical or High security risk.

Critical or High residual risk remains a release blocker by default.

## Contribution controls

External contributions remain disabled until DCO enforcement, review rules, branch protection, and a private vulnerability-reporting channel are configured and verified.

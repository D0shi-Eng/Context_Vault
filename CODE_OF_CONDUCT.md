# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, technically rigorous, and harassment-free project environment.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence without attacking people.
- Be precise about what is implemented, tested, assumed, or unverified.
- Report security concerns privately.
- Respect privacy, licensing, attribution, and contribution ownership.
- Accept review as part of producing dependable software.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or targeted abuse.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Knowingly submitting copied, malicious, backdoored, or credential-bearing code.
- Misrepresenting test, security, benchmark, or authorship evidence.
- Publicly disclosing an unremediated vulnerability before coordinated disclosure.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content, reject contributions, restrict participation, or report conduct to the relevant platform when necessary to protect the project and its participants.
